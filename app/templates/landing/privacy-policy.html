Privacy Policy

YourSelf Media (“YourSelf Media,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of our customers, users, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, and disclose information when you visit our website at https://yourselfmedia.in or use our ClickTracker platform and related services.

This Privacy Policy also explains your rights regarding your Personal Data and how you can contact us regarding privacy-related questions or requests.

By accessing our website, creating an account, or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the practices described here.

Consent

By accessing or using the YourSelf Media ClickTracker website and services, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should not access or use the website, ClickTracker platform, or related services.

Where applicable law requires consent for specific types of data processing, YourSelf Media will seek the appropriate consent before carrying out such processing.

Personal Information We Collect

YourSelf Media may collect information that you provide directly when you interact with our website, create an account, contact us, subscribe to our services, submit forms, request information, or use the ClickTracker platform.

Depending on how you interact with our services, the information we collect may include your name, company name, company address, telephone number, mobile telephone number, email address, billing information, payment information, invoice details, account information, and other information necessary to provide our services.

We may also collect information that you voluntarily provide when communicating with our support team or submitting questions, feedback, reviews, suggestions, or other information through the website.

Use of Personal Information

YourSelf Media uses Personal Data to operate, maintain, and improve the ClickTracker platform and to provide requested services.

We may use your information to create and manage your account, process payments, provide customer support, communicate with you about your account, provide requested services, maintain platform security, prevent fraud, troubleshoot technical problems, and improve our products and services.

Where permitted by applicable law, we may also use your name and email address to send service-related communications, company updates, product information, and marketing communications concerning services that may be relevant to you.

You may opt out of promotional communications by using the unsubscribe option included in applicable marketing emails or by contacting us at hello@yourselfmedia.com.

Log Data, Cookies and Similar Technologies

When you interact with the YourSelf Media website or ClickTracker platform, we may automatically collect certain information through cookies, log files, pixels, and similar technologies.

These technologies may help us understand how visitors use our website, improve website functionality, personalize content, maintain security, analyze performance, and measure the effectiveness of our services and marketing activities.

The information collected through these technologies may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, device identifiers, language preferences, network information, referring URLs, pages viewed, access times, clickstream information, and other technical information associated with your interaction with our website or services.

Where appropriate, this information may be analyzed in an aggregated or non-identifiable form for analytics, performance measurement, security, and service improvement.

Cookies

YourSelf Media may use cookies and similar technologies to provide essential website functionality, remember preferences, maintain user sessions, analyze website traffic, improve performance, and support security.

Some cookies may be provided by third-party service providers that assist us with analytics, advertising, payment processing, customer support, or other business functions.

You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the availability or functionality of some features of the website or ClickTracker platform.

Where legally required, YourSelf Media will provide appropriate cookie consent and management options.

Analytics and Tracking Technologies

YourSelf Media may use analytics and tracking technologies to understand website usage, evaluate platform performance, identify technical issues, and improve the overall user experience.

These technologies may collect information about how you interact with the website, including pages visited, links clicked, browser information, device information, approximate location, and other technical usage information.

We may use aggregated information generated through these technologies to understand general website traffic and service performance.

Social Media Features

The YourSelf Media website may include social media features, sharing tools, or links to social networking platforms.

When you interact with a social media feature, the relevant third-party provider may collect information about your interaction according to its own privacy policy.

YourSelf Media does not control the privacy practices of third-party social media providers. You should review the privacy policies of those providers to understand how they collect, use, and process your information.

Third-Party Service Providers

YourSelf Media may work with third-party service providers to operate and support the website and ClickTracker platform.

These providers may assist with services such as cloud hosting, data storage, database management, payment processing, analytics, customer support, security, communications, and other business-related functions.

Third-party service providers may receive access to Personal Data only to the extent reasonably necessary to perform services on our behalf.

YourSelf Media expects relevant service providers to protect Personal Data and use it only for the purposes for which it has been provided or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Disclosure of Personal Data

YourSelf Media may disclose Personal Data to service providers, contractors, technology providers, payment processors, hosting providers, analytics providers, and other partners where such disclosure is necessary to provide, maintain, secure, or improve our services.

We may also disclose Personal Data where necessary to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, court orders, government requests, or other lawful requirements.

Where legally permitted, we may take reasonable steps to notify you of a legally required disclosure.

Business Transfers

If YourSelf Media is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, financing, sale of assets, corporate restructuring, dissolution, or similar transaction, Personal Data may be transferred as part of the relevant business assets.

Where required by applicable law, we will take appropriate measures concerning the handling of Personal Data during such transactions.

Any successor organization may continue to process Personal Data in accordance with applicable law and the terms of the applicable privacy policy.

Employee Access

Access to Personal Data within YourSelf Media is limited to employees, contractors, and authorized personnel who require such access to perform their responsibilities or provide services.

Authorized personnel are expected to handle Personal Data confidentially and in accordance with applicable privacy and security requirements.

Data Retention

YourSelf Media retains Personal Data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain your account, fulfill legitimate business purposes, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, prevent fraud, and enforce applicable agreements.

The length of time information is retained may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected, the nature of the service, and applicable legal requirements.

Where Personal Data is no longer required for legitimate business or legal purposes, we may delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it.

In certain circumstances, information may be retained for a longer period where necessary to protect our legitimate interests, comply with legal requirements, investigate potential violations, or resolve disputes.

Data Security

YourSelf Media maintains reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational safeguards designed to protect Personal Data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Where appropriate, we may use encryption and other security technologies to protect information transmitted through our website and services.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Although we take reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee that the website, ClickTracker platform, or information stored through our services will be completely protected against every possible security threat, unauthorized access, malware, hacking attempt, or security breach.

Links to Other Websites

The YourSelf Media website or ClickTracker platform may contain links to third-party websites, services, applications, or platforms.

These third-party websites operate independently from YourSelf Media and may have their own privacy policies and data practices.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to information collected by third-party websites that we do not control.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing them with Personal Data.

Social Media and Widgets

Our website may include social media buttons, widgets, sharing features, or other third-party integrations.

These features may collect information such as your IP address, browser information, the page you are visiting, and other technical information.

Third-party features may use cookies or similar technologies to function properly.

Your interactions with these features are governed by the privacy policies of the companies providing them. YourSelf Media does not control the information collected through third-party social media features and is not responsible for their independent privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

The YourSelf Media website and ClickTracker platform are not intended for individuals under the age of eighteen.

We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from individuals under eighteen years of age.

If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from an individual under eighteen without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information from our systems, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your Personal Data.

These rights may include requesting access to Personal Data, correcting inaccurate information, requesting deletion, restricting certain processing activities, objecting to certain processing, or withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise an applicable privacy right or ask a question about your Personal Data, you may contact YourSelf Media at hello@yourselfmedia.com.

We will review and process valid requests in accordance with applicable law.

Where applicable law provides such rights, you may request that YourSelf Media restrict the processing of your Personal Data or object to certain processing activities.
